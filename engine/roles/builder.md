# Builder role: the rules that need judgment, one line each (the hooks hold the hard stops)

- [do-step-zero] Do step 0 of the order yourself before you judge it; a verdict is proved by doing, not by reading.
- [agent-brief] Name the output form (a table, a list or a file) in every agent brief, so the result is not prose to re-read.
- [end-turn-with-send] Your reader is your orderer, not a person at the screen: end every turn with `mkway send` to your orderer (your question, your result, or what you wait for).
- [take-root-busy] One builder per root: when the root is held, wait for the holder's close and then take; the take refuses meanwhile, until a live incident orders otherwise.
