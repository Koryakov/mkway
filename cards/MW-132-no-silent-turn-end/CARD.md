## 1. Goal

«At the FIRST stop of a turn with no handoff to its orderer, the ENGINE writes a notice into the orderer's inbox at once, carrying the session's last message, and blocks once asking only for the ping line» — chief, R337, R338, 2026-11-17. A builder that ends a turn with a question in its own chat and no `mkway send` leaves its orderer with an idle session and no word; the refusal delivery of MW-128 fires only after a gate refusal, so this case was silent. This card widens MW-128's Stop step in its own shape.

Terms (the order's words). **Handoff**: a `mkway send` by this session to its ORDERER, recorded in the message record (`send` rows), strictly later than the turn's boundary row (form 2 of R338 2: see the Log hole). **Turn** (R340a): the calls of the session since its boundary. Only the Stop rows of the `stop` group our step writes open and close turns (each keeps the hook input's `stop_hook_active` flag; a row with no flag reads as false); a prompt row never does, because a peer message that arrives mid-turn writes one. A stop with the flag true continues the turn of the stop before it (the task has two Stop hooks and this step cannot know which one blocked). A stop also continues the turn of the stop before it when that stop was BLOCKED BY THIS STEP (its own record row says so; R342): a blocked stop cannot end a turn, because the flag may be false at the stop after the block and the ping and the rule would loop. The boundary is the Stop row just before the first stop of the current chain of continued stops; with no such row it is the session's first row. No row at all, or no prompt row anywhere (the engine saw no turn begin): nothing is judged, one record row `handoff-no-turn-start`. **Handoff notice**: the inbox row the engine writes. **Delivered for this turn**: a mark of this session later than the turn's boundary: the record row `handoff-notice` (it also says `blocked`), `handoff-no-orderer`, a MW-128 record row (`refusal-delivered`, `refusal-no-orderer`), or an error row of the calls log (`handoff-notice-failed`, `refusal-delivery-failed`); the notice row keeps the boundary time as `turn`.

Implementation choices (the order leaves them): the pure decision is the new module `engine/core_handoff.py`; the writer is `messages.send_handoff` (one inbox `notice` row from the session to the orderer, no topology check: the engine's own write, so an orderer that is a seen session but no lead still gets it, R338a); its message record is the event `handoff-notice`, which `messages.sends_by` does not count (R1-3); the reader of this session's sends with their recipients is `messages.sends_to`; the reader of (card, orderer) is `messages.held_card`, which raises on a failed register read; the boundary reader is `gatemode.handoff_turn`; the block is given only when the session's role is `builder` (its orderer a registered lead), a session with another orderer gets the notice and no block (R338a).
