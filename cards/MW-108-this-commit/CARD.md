## 1. Goal

«we should have straight, simple as its possible mkway process - it should help work on tasks - not be as evil dog - it should be strong helper!» — owner, 2026-11-16 (R263). Concept lines 2 and 5, kind K2: a gate judges only what this commit changes, and a new gate never breaks a card already in progress. Help it gives the task: a red test that was already red at HEAD, in no card's Paths, or in the card's own Paths and recorded red at its take, no longer stops a commit that adds no new red; it is shown with its owner. A red inside a card's Paths at `card take` is recorded (from the nearest record of HEAD's history, never a suite run) as that card's inherited red and must be green at the orderer's close; when the record cannot be established, the take says so and every red in the card's Paths counts as new (R283); the orderer may release an inherited red id to another named open card (`card release-red <id> --to <card>`). The merge (K5) and the install keep the full-green rule. Replays: DD-001 (a green commit refused for the old red of a closed card, 14.5 h) and DD-010 (a red-first commit refused for an old red of its own card).
