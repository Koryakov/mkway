## 1. Goal

«we should have straight, simple as its possible mkway process - it should help work on tasks - not be as evil dog - it should be strong helper!» — owner, 2026-11-16 (R263). Order of the operator: ruling R270d. Concept lines 2 and 3, kind K2: a gate judges only what this action changes, and a recorded red test that now passes must be seen as passed, whatever characters its parameter part holds. Help it gives the task: a test parametrized with raw strings (spaces, semicolons, quotes) no longer makes the commit gate say «deleted or renamed» when the test passes. R271b widens it to all readers: the `-v` status line, the `--collect-only -q` line, the `FAILED` and `ERROR` summary lines of the commit gate, and the `FAILED` list of the close refusal.
