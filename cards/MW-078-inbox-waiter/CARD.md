How to use: this card follows `cards/_TEMPLATE/CARD.md`; written by hand, as `card new` cannot run in the engine
repository. `VERSION` is not changed here. There is no refusal: the common-rule items for refusals (`tests.<key>.mode`,
reason ids, an e2e deny) do not apply; the switch is `wake.mode` on or off. The waiter is a Stop handler that never
blocks and never denies: it wakes a session or it exits 0.

## 1. Goal

«I cant list all your sessions to cjeck that someone want my action!!!» and «it should be implemented like sending
messages we discussed 20 minutes ago! not only text!!!!» — owner, 2026-11-12. An idle lead or builder is woken by a new
row in its inbox (a result, a question, an order) with the row's line, and goes on; every other session is left alone.

## Acceptance examples

Each row becomes a test; `card close` needs every row to name one. E1 to E25 are the pure core, S1 to S6 are real
`hook-wait` processes with a deadline of 3 s and a poll of 1 s, R1 to R19 run the waiter in this process with a clock
that never waits, P1 is the replay, X1 to X9 go through a real install and the real command line, K1 to K11 and D1 to
D5 are the keys, the manifest and the README; R20 to R26 and I1 to I8 were added after the green code, from the coverage
check (the install core and the failure edges of the waiter). The waiter keeps `<state.dir>/wake/<session>.pid` and
`<state.dir>/wake/<session>.woken` (one row id a line). The hooks documentation does not say that `asyncRewake` is
honoured on Stop: the live check (L1 to L3) must prove it.
