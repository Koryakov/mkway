## 1. Goal

Sessions that order work for builders have no status anywhere: no register, no card, no load view. A lead becomes a
role with a clear boundary: it is known by its session id, it may order, write the order and hand cards on, it may not
edit or hold a card, and the engine tells it what needs it (a card past its Appetite, a gate that tripped, a default
taken, a builder finished) in an inbox it must acknowledge, with the owner seeing what stays unacknowledged — order of
2026-11-11.
