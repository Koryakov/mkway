## 1. Goal

Sessions message each other with no order: one body to two recipients, an order relayed through two hops and rewritten,
statuses to the owner from several sessions, a peer's words taken for authority. «yes, multiple sending messages instead
of single recipient - this is problem … maybe we should have some bus of messages, maybe straight connections, I dont
kno - but here is anarchy now, we need clear order» — owner, 2026-11-11.

The order is a star per card: a lead and the builders of the cards it ordered, a builder to its lead only, lead to
lead, a session with no role to a lead only. Every message is a row in one append-only inbox per session, written only by
`mkway send`; the session pinged is told with the native tool, by a ping line the hook gate checks; the owner reads one
board.
