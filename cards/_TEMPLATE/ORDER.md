# <ID>: order

The order a lead gives for one card, written before the builder starts. The card form stays as it is; this file holds
what the builder needs to start without asking.

## Objective

What the work must achieve, in one or two plain sentences.

## Output form

What the builder hands back: the files, the card Log rows, and a message of kind `result` when the work is finished.

## Time-box

The most time the work is worth, and the point at which the builder stops and says so.

## Boundary

What is in scope, which paths may be edited, and what the builder must not touch or decide.
