Scope: the DECISIONS of the lock gate (`gate_lock.py`, `locks.py`). The shell-command reader cases of the MW-003 hardening
modules and the start-line cases stay where they are. The pure decision is `core_lock.decide(facts)`; it asks for a fact it
does not have with a `need` verdict, the shell reads that fact and decides again. Behaviour is unchanged: the old tests of
the gate passed before and after the move (Log).

## 1. Goal

«only fix that tests! go! faster as possible!» — owner, 2026-11-12. The tests of the lock family become fast and true, with
the same behaviour proven: decisions in pure table tests, fact readers on real files and real git, the real hook for one
deny per reason (the existing end-to-end files, unchanged).
