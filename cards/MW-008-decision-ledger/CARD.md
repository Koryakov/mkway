## 1. Goal

«Decision ledger: search before asking» — owner, 2026-11-10, in the order that carries this card.
A session asks the owner only after it has searched the past decisions. The engine gets a ledger file
(`<bundle.root>/ledger/ledger.md`) with `mkway ledger add`, a search over the ledger and the knowledge files with
`mkway search`, a gate that refuses a question before a search of this turn, a stronger ask gate (a substance ask
carries the ledger count and a ledger quote, or a zero count and the search record), and a start block line.
