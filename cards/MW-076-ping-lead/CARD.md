How to use: this card follows `cards/_TEMPLATE/CARD.md`; written by hand, as `card new` cannot run in the engine
repository. `VERSION` is not changed here. Gate `ping` (class form), entry `ping.stop`, profile switch
`gates.ping.mode` (`trip` default, `watch`). Reason ids: `ping-not-sent` (the refusal), `ping-skipped` (the log verdict
of the second Stop and the record row), `ping-skipped-listed` (the lead's warn line).

## 1. Goal

«A builder that reports to its lead also pings it: a builder's Stop is refused once when the turn caused an inbox write to
another session and no SendMessage followed; the refusal carries the exact ping line and the target's name; the second
Stop passes and a record row is written; the lead's Stop lists each skipped ping once.» — rulings R185 to R188, 2026-11-12.
